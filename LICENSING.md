  Confirm the artist's redistribution terms or replace it before redistributing
  a fully free-asset version of the game.

  project's MIT-licensed tooling. Third-party source-art inputs retain their own
  terms; regenerating an asset does not remove those terms.
- **Redot web runtime:** MIT, with additional bundled library licenses. The

  commit `4f5b14aba`. Web exports include these notices and Freedoom attribution
  on their Credits & licenses page. Refresh the engine notices when switching
  to a different engine/template version.
